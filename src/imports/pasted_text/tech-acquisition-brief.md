Create a premium, modern, high-end B2B technology acquisition and technology services website.

The company is a technology-focused acquisition and strategic services firm. It helps organizations identify, evaluate, acquire, integrate, and strategically partner with technology companies, software products, AI platforms, data platforms, intellectual property, and specialized technology capabilities.

The website must feel like a combination of:

* Premium technology company
* Strategic M&A / acquisition firm
* Enterprise consulting company
* Modern AI company
* High-end investment/advisory brand

Do NOT make it look like a generic SaaS landing page.
Do NOT use a template-like design.
Do NOT overload the interface with gradients, glowing effects, or unnecessary cards.
The design should feel sophisticated, trustworthy, technical, intelligent, and premium.

==================================================

1. OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION
   ==================================================

Create a responsive website optimized primarily for desktop at 1440px width, while also designing responsive behavior for tablet and mobile.

Visual personality:

* Premium
* Minimal
* Editorial
* Enterprise
* Technology-driven
* Confident
* Sophisticated
* Clean
* High trust

Use a restrained color palette:

* Deep near-black / charcoal as the primary dark color
* Warm white / off-white backgrounds
* Neutral gray typography
* Very subtle accent color for important interactive elements
* Avoid excessive bright colors

Typography:

* Use a modern premium sans-serif typeface
* Large editorial headlines
* Tight but readable typography
* Strong hierarchy
* Large numbers
* Small uppercase labels with letter spacing
* Body copy should remain highly readable

Use generous whitespace.

Grid:

* 12-column desktop grid
* Maximum content width around 1200–1280px
* Consistent 24–32px spacing system
* Large vertical spacing between major sections

Corners:

* Moderate rounded corners
* Avoid overly rounded "startup UI" cards
* Use approximately 12–20px radius where appropriate

Shadows:

* Very subtle
* Prefer borders and contrast over heavy shadows

17. SCROLL EXPERIENCE
=====================

This is extremely important.

The entire website should feel like one continuous premium story.

Implement:

* Smooth scrolling
* Scroll-triggered reveal animations
* Subtle parallax
* Sticky sections where appropriate
* Horizontal storytelling section for Services
* Progressive text reveals
* Image/visual parallax
* Background transitions
* Scale transitions
* Fade + translate combinations
* Sticky navigation transformation
* Section progress indicators

Animation principles:

Duration:
400–800ms for UI transitions.

Use:
ease-out / cubic-bezier style motion.

Avoid:

* Fast bouncing
* Excessive rotations
* Large zooms
* Constant floating animations
* Distracting particle effects
* Overly flashy transitions

Animations should communicate hierarchy and depth.

==================================================
18. PARALLAX SYSTEM
===================

Use 3 visual depth layers:

BACKGROUND:
Very slow movement.

MIDDLE:
Moderate movement.

FOREGROUND:
Normal scroll movement.

Parallax should be subtle:
approximately 10–40px movement.

Do not make text difficult to read.

For major hero visual:

* Background grid moves at approximately 0.2x scroll speed
* Middle objects at approximately 0.4x
* Foreground visual at approximately 0.7x
