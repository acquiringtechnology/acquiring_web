==================================================
PRIMARY CONVERSION JOURNEY
==========================

Design the entire website around one primary conversion journey:

DISCOVER
↓
UNDERSTAND
↓
BUILD TRUST
↓
CHECK FIT
↓
SUBMIT TECHNOLOGY
↓
START A CONFIDENTIAL CONVERSATION

The primary conversion is:

"SUBMIT YOUR TECHNOLOGY"

The secondary conversion is:

"TALK TO OUR ACQUISITION TEAM"

Every major section should naturally move the visitor closer to one of these two actions.

Do not make every section aggressively sales-focused. The experience should progressively build confidence before asking for the submission.

==================================================

1. STAGE ONE — DISCOVER
   ==================================================

Goal:
Immediately communicate what the company does.

Hero headline:

"WE ACQUIRE
TECHNOLOGY.
WE BUILD WHAT
COMES NEXT."

Supporting message:

"We partner with technology companies, founders, and organizations to acquire, evaluate, integrate, and unlock the long-term potential of valuable technology."

Primary CTA:

"SUBMIT YOUR TECHNOLOGY"

Secondary CTA:

"EXPLORE OUR CAPABILITIES"

The primary CTA should be visually dominant.

Hero CTA interaction:

* Arrow moves slightly on hover
* Button background transitions smoothly
* Button has subtle elevation
* No excessive glow
* Smooth 300–400ms transition

The visitor should understand within 5 seconds:

WHAT:
Technology acquisition and strategic technology services.

WHO:
Technology companies, founders, and organizations.

WHY:
To create long-term value from technology.

ACTION:
Submit a technology opportunity.

==================================================
2. STAGE TWO — UNDERSTAND
=========================

After the hero, explain what "technology" means to the company.

Use the statement:

"Technology is more than a product."

Explain that the company evaluates:

* Technology
* Intellectual property
* Product
* Data
* Customers
* Team
* Market opportunity
* Strategic value

The purpose of this section is to make the visitor think:

"They understand the full value of what we've built."

Use scroll-triggered typography reveals.

Do not show a CTA immediately.

Allow the visitor to absorb the positioning.

==================================================
3. STAGE THREE — BUILD TRUST
============================

Use the "What We Acquire" and "Our Services" sections to establish expertise.

The visitor should be able to quickly recognize their technology category.

Categories:

Software & SaaS
AI & Machine Learning
Data & Analytics
Healthcare Technology
Enterprise Technology
Intellectual Property

Then demonstrate capabilities:

Technology Acquisition
Technical Due Diligence
Technology Valuation
Technology Integration
Strategic Partnerships

Create a visual progression:

WHAT YOU BUILT
→
HOW WE UNDERSTAND IT
→
HOW WE CREATE VALUE

Use sophisticated scroll interactions.

When the user scrolls through the service section, maintain the feeling that the company has a structured methodology rather than simply buying companies.

==================================================
4. STAGE FOUR — CHECK FIT
=========================

Create a dedicated "IS THIS A FIT?" section.

Heading:

"COULD WE BE THE RIGHT PARTNER?"

Supporting copy:

"We evaluate technology based on strategic fit, technical quality, intellectual property, market opportunity, product maturity, and long-term potential."

Create an interactive qualification checklist.

Criteria:

01 — Technology
Is there meaningful proprietary technology?

02 — IP
Is the intellectual property owned or transferable?

03 — Product
Is there a working product or technology capability?

04 — Market
Is there a meaningful market opportunity?

05 — Strategic Fit
Does the technology align with our acquisition or partnership objectives?

06 — Scalability
Can the technology create value at greater scale?

Add a subtle interaction:

"CHECK YOUR FIT →"

When clicked, open a lightweight qualification panel rather than immediately displaying a long form.

==================================================
5. STAGE FIVE — QUALIFICATION
=============================

Create a short, frictionless qualification experience.

Do not ask for excessive information initially.

Step 1:

"What are you looking to discuss?"

Options:

Technology Acquisition
Technology Partnership
Technology Licensing
Technical Due Diligence
Technology Valuation
Other

Step 2:

"What have you built?"

Options:

SaaS / Software
AI / Machine Learning
Data / Analytics
Healthcare Technology
Enterprise Technology
Other

Step 3:

"Tell us briefly about the opportunity."

Short text field.

Step 4:

"Continue to Confidential Submission"

CTA:

"CONTINUE"

Use a progress indicator:

01 / 03
02 / 03
03 / 03

The interface should feel simple and premium.

Do not make the user feel like they are applying for funding.

==================================================
7. FORM DESIGN
==============

Make the form feel like a premium private conversation.

Do NOT use a generic SaaS contact form.

Use:

* Large labels
* Spacious inputs
* Clear field hierarchy
* Minimal borders
* Strong focus states
* Inline validation
* Smooth transitions
* Upload area with clear instructions

Desktop:
Two-column form layout where appropriate.

Mobile:
Single-column layout.

The primary submit button should remain visually prominent.

==================================================

SUBMISSION RECEIVED

01

02
Initial strategic review

03
Follow-up conversation

04
Opportunity assessment

CTA:

"RETURN TO WEBSITE"

Secondary:

"EXPLORE OUR INSIGHTS"

==================================================
9. SECONDARY CONVERSION — TALK TO OUR TEAM
==========================================

At every major decision point, provide a secondary path for visitors who are not ready to submit detailed information.

CTA:

"TALK TO OUR ACQUISITION TEAM"

This should open a simpler contact experience.

Fields:

Name
Company
Email
Message

CTA:

"START A CONVERSATION"

This path is especially important for:

* Strategic partners
* Investors
* Advisors
* Enterprise organizations
* Visitors who want an initial discussion

12. SMART CTA TRANSITIONS
=========================

Use contextual CTA language.

If the visitor is in "What We Acquire":

"SEE IF YOUR TECHNOLOGY FITS →"

If the visitor is in "Services":

"DISCUSS AN OPPORTUNITY →"

If the visitor is in "What We Look For":

"CHECK YOUR FIT →"

If the visitor reaches the Founder section:

"SUBMIT YOUR TECHNOLOGY →"

At the final section:

"START A CONFIDENTIAL CONVERSATION →"

All of these ultimately lead toward the same conversion ecosystem.

==================================================
14. CONVERSION PSYCHOLOGY
=========================

The page should answer these questions in order:

1. WHAT DO YOU DO?
   Technology acquisition and strategic services.

2. DO YOU UNDERSTAND MY TECHNOLOGY?
   Demonstrate technical expertise.

3. DO YOU UNDERSTAND BUSINESS VALUE?
   Show valuation, market, strategic fit, and integration capabilities.

4. WHAT TYPES OF TECHNOLOGY DO YOU CONSIDER?
   Show acquisition categories.

5. HOW DOES THE PROCESS WORK?
   Show the five-step process.

6. WOULD MY TECHNOLOGY BE A FIT?
   Show acquisition criteria.

7. WHAT HAPPENS IF I CONTACT YOU?
   Explain the confidential submission process.

8. WHAT SHOULD I DO NOW?
   Submit Your Technology.

==================================================
15. FINAL CONVERSION PRINCIPLE
==============================

The website should never feel like it is forcing the visitor to convert.

Instead, create a progressive narrative:

"WE UNDERSTAND TECHNOLOGY."

↓

"WE UNDERSTAND ITS STRATEGIC VALUE."

↓

"WE HAVE A CLEAR ACQUISITION PROCESS."

↓

"WE KNOW WHAT WE LOOK FOR."

↓

"YOUR TECHNOLOGY MAY FIT."

↓

"LET'S HAVE A CONFIDENTIAL CONVERSATION."

↓

"SUBMIT YOUR TECHNOLOGY."

The final emotional state should be:

"I trust them enough to start a conversation."

The primary conversion is NOT simply submitting a form.

The real conversion is:

TRUST
→
QUALIFIED INTEREST
→
CONFIDENTIAL CONVERSATION
→
TECHNOLOGY EVALUATION
→
ACQUISITION / PARTNERSHIP
